OSD32MP15x Carrier

A custom four-layer Linux single-board computer built around the Octavo Systems OSD32MP157C system-in-package. Derived from the vendor reference design and reduced to a targeted feature set on a 134.6 by 76.2 mm board, designed in KiCad 10.

When
2026, routed and rule-checked
Role
Solo: schematic reduction, placement, routing, design rules
Stack
KiCad 10, Octavo OSD32MP157C (STM32MP157C, 512 MB DDR3, STPMIC1)

Why a system-in-package

The OSD32MP157C is a 324-ball package that combines an STM32MP157C, with its dual Arm Cortex-A7 cores and a Cortex-M4, together with 512 MB of DDR3 and an STPMIC1 power management IC. Putting the memory inside the package removes length-matched DDR3 routing from the carrier entirely. That is what makes a four-layer board viable at this density: the hardest routing problem on a Linux SBC is already solved before the first track is drawn.

Reduced, not redrawn

The design began as the Octavo OSD32MP1-RED reference and was cut down rather than drawn from scratch. The three-phase motor-control interface, DCMI camera port, MIPI-DSI channel, and expansion headers all came out, along with the level shifters, analog sense chains, and pull-ups that existed only to serve them.

The careful part was verifying that nothing retained depended on what was removed. Several SiP pins are muxed between the motor bank and the display bus, so each deletion meant checking the pin table again before the net could go.

The board

Board134.6 by 76.2 mm, four-layer FR4
StackSignal, ground plane, 3V3 plane, signal
Components295 placed, 129 of them on the back face
Routing413 nets, 7,105 segments, 981 vias, 50 copper pours
Design rules5 mil clearance and track width, 18/10 mil vias
ProcessNo via-in-pad, no blind or buried vias. Standard fab capability

Interfaces

  • HDMI, SiI9022
  • 10/100 Ethernet, KSZ8051 with magjack
  • Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, Murata LBEE5KL1DX
  • USB-C with STUSB1600 PD controller
  • USB 2.0
  • CAN, TJA1441
  • 4 GB eMMC
  • microSD
  • Tag-Connect SWD/JTAG
  • Buffered UART console

Status

Routed and rule-checked. Stackup finalisation and differential-pair impedance tuning are pending before fabrication.

Placement and routing derived from the Octavo Systems OSD32MP1-RED reference design.
